FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Poshaarya different from other fitness apps?

Poshaarya is built specifically for Indian users with 100,000+ Indian food items, regional cuisine, restaurant foods, and packaged foods. Our AI provides personalized recommendations based on Indian dietary patterns and lifestyle.

Is Poshaarya free to use?

Yes! We offer a generous free tier with basic tracking features. Premium plans unlock advanced analytics, AI insights, meal planning, and more.

Can I track regional Indian foods?

Absolutely! Our database covers North Indian, South Indian, East Indian, and West Indian cuisines. From dosa to dhokla, biryani to bhel puri - everything is covered with accurate nutrition data.

How does the AI-powered meal planning work?

Our AI analyzes your goals, dietary preferences, activity level, past meals, and nutritional needs to create personalized meal plans. It learns from your preferences over time and gets smarter.

Is my data safe and private?

Yes! We take security seriously. All data is encrypted, we follow industry best practices, and we never share your personal health data with third parties.
